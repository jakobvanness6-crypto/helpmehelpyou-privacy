Help Me Help You: privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Help Me Help You is a Windows app that reads information about your computer and saves it as a report file, so you can share it with whoever helps you with your PC.

What the app reads

To build the report, the app reads: Windows version, processor, memory, graphics card, PC make and model, drive sizes, free space and health status, names of programs that start with Windows, Windows update dates and failures, the total size of common folders (totals only, never file or folder names), battery wear on laptops, and counts of recent crashes and errors together with the names of programs that crashed.

What happens to it

The report is saved as a file on your computer, normally on your Desktop. The app does not send the report, or any other information, anywhere. It does not connect to the internet and has no analytics or tracking. Whether to share the report, and with whom, is entirely up to you.

What the app leaves out

The app does not read your documents, photos, browsing history or passwords, and it does not read the text of Windows event logs. It tries to leave out your username, your computer's name and serial numbers. If any of these appear by accident, the app replaces them with placeholders before saving the report.

Removing it

Uninstall the app from Windows Settings, under Apps. To remove a report, delete the report file from your Desktop.
